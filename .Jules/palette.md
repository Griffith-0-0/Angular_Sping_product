## 2026-07-26 - Prevent Layout Shift with Absolute Positioning for Focused Skip Links
**Learning:** Skip-to-content links styled with Bootstrap's `.visually-hidden-focusable` can enter the standard static document flow upon keyboard focus. This causes a major page-wide layout shift that pushes all subsequent elements down. Aside from the jarring visual experience for keyboard users, this layout shift introduces race conditions in automated end-to-end testing (like Playwright), causing click interactions to miss their targets due to coordinate shifts during focus/blur transitions.
**Action:** Always absolutely position skip-to-content links when focused using `position: absolute !important` (with appropriate `top`, `left`, and a high `z-index`) to overlay them cleanly over the content instead of shifting the layout.
