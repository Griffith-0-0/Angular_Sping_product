## 2026-04-03 - [Semantic Navigation & Active States]
**Learning:** Using `btn-group` with `routerLinkActive` and `ariaCurrentWhenActive` provides a cohesive visual and programmatic experience for tab-like navigation.
**Action:** Always wrap navigation in `<nav aria-label="...">` and use `<a>` tags for better accessibility compared to `<div>` buttons.
